The challenge

Schools run on disconnected tools.

Data silos

Student info, grades and fees live in separate spreadsheets and apps — no single source of truth.

Manual, error-prone work

Paper attendance, hand-calculated report cards and manual fee reconciliation waste staff time.

Poor parent visibility

Parents chase phone calls and office visits to learn about grades, attendance and fees.

Compliance risk

India's DPDP Act 2023 requires verifiable parental consent and strict handling of children's data.

The solution

Scholr brings it all together.

A modern, integrated platform that automates high-volume workflows, gives every stakeholder self-service access, and is private and compliant from day one — so staff spend less time on admin and more time on students.

  • One centralised student record (SIS) as the backbone of everything
  • Automated attendance, grading and fee collection
  • Self-service portals for admins, teachers, students and parents
  • Actionable analytics that flag at-risk students 6–8 weeks earlier

One platform, four portals

Everyone gets exactly what they need.

Role-based access means each person sees only the modules relevant to them — enforced at the service and data layers, least-privilege by default.

A

Administrator

Oversight dashboards, configuration, user & role management, and every module school-wide.

T

Teacher

Take attendance, manage the gradebook, post assignments and message parents.

S

Student

Timetable, homework, grades, attendance, study material and announcements.

P

Parent

Child's grades, attendance and fees (pay online), live bus tracking and teacher chat.

Web-first & responsive

Works beautifully on a parent's phone browser.

No app install required. WhatsApp is the primary parent channel, with SMS and email built in.
